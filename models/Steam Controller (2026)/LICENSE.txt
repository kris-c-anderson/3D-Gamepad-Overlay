Steam Controller (2026) model
=============================

Adapted from the official Steam Controller CAD files released by Valve Corporation:
https://gitlab.steamos.cloud/SteamHardware/SteamController

Original material (c) Valve Corporation, licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes: the single CAD solid was split into separately moving parts (buttons,
sticks, trackpads, bumpers, triggers, grip buttons), openings were capped, the
mesh was decimated and re-scaled, and overlay metadata (info.txt) was added.
